Docs

P500
1 READA keeper reads every new Periodic Transaction Report from the House Clerk and Senate eFD, including scanned paper filings.
2 POSTEach trade line is posted on Solana with the doc id and a SHA-256 hash of the PDF, so anyone can check it against the source.
3 CALLCallers copy or fade a line, pick a horizon (7, 30 or 90 days) and conviction. Calls lock at the filing's posted time.
4 SETTLEAt the horizon the call settles against an on-chain price oracle. Points stack weekly; top callers split the pool.
